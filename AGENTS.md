# Permanent city design rule

The city must visibly appear alive, bustling, full of people and construction as
measured data builds it. This is a permanent product requirement.

- Background artwork supports the dynamic evolving city. Do not substitute a
  static panorama or skyline fade for the live city experience.
- People, working crews, cranes, machinery and deliveries must be clearly visible
  and distributed across the city at normal desktop and compact card sizes.
- Lifetime measured downloads control city growth and construction progress.
  Ambient residents may stay active while construction waits for data.
- Preserve animation timelines across polling, growth, sorting and resizing.
- Honor the user's pause, motion setting and reduced-motion preference.
- Verify visible motion and construction progression in the rendered UI; checks
  that merely count animation nodes do not establish that the city feels alive.

# Artwork rule

- Use imagegen for all new or replacement illustrated artwork, including small
  scenery and sky assets. Match the established detailed watercolor style.
- Do not substitute hand-drawn SVG shapes or emoji-style icons for artwork.
- Sky visitors are subtle, rare, randomly timed easter eggs, with long quiet gaps.
