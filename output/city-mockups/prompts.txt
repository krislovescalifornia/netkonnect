netKonnect city mockups — concept artwork
Generation: built-in imagegen. Preview concepts; application code is unchanged.

Shared concept
Minimal colorful line art on white. Thin dark blue-gray outlines, restrained lime,
teal, sky blue, warm coral and ochre fills, abundant whitespace. Each service has
a tiny settlement that grows with cumulative recorded download + upload bytes.
Current traffic controls supply vehicle activity. Bicycles become wood pushcarts;
trucks become pickups carrying planks and crates; planes drop parachute crates.
Data volumes shown in artwork are illustrative. Preserve download/upload direction.

01 — Compact service cities
A polished desktop netKonnect dashboard concept with a narrow left sidebar,
three roomy service rows, application labels on the left, dual traffic lanes in
the center, and a miniature city beside each service on the right. Show YouTube
as metropolis at 128 GB, OneDrive as village at 8.4 GB, and GitHub as house at
180 MB. Include tiny wood pushcarts, loaded pickups, and a plane dropping supplies
near the largest city. Use bright sparse flat color accents and crisp fine outlines.
Keep real totals readable. Title: Service cities. Subtitle: Built from total data.
Footer: Illustrative data. A clean readable UI, not a toy game or dense illustration.

02 — Growth and supplies
A spacious landscape concept sheet titled Every byte builds. Seven evenly spaced
miniature settlements in a clear labeled sequence: Shack, House, Mansion,
Neighborhood, Village, City, Metropolis. Same thin dark blue-gray line art and
restrained lime, teal, blue, coral and ochre accents throughout. Architecture grows
in both density and height. No numerical thresholds. Below the progression, show
three larger illustrations: a small two-wheel hand pushcart piled with wooden
planks; a pickup with an open bed full of crates, timber and building supplies;
a propeller cargo plane releasing a crate on a parachute above a tiny building.
Labels: Pushcart, Supply pickup, Airdrop. Bottom note: City size = total data.
Vehicle activity = current traffic. Mostly white, generous gaps, minimal detail.

03 — Service dioramas
A netKonnect dashboard concept titled Service cities, using three horizontally
stacked spacious service cards. Each card has a simple application icon and name
at left, two thin supply traffic lanes through the middle, and a small colorful
line-art settlement occupying the right third beside the service name and total.
YouTube / 128 GB / Metropolis: tall slender towers and an airplane dropping two
parachute supply crates. OneDrive / 8.4 GB / Village: small cottages, a shop,
trees, and a loaded supply pickup. GitHub / 180 MB / House: one simple house,
wood stack, and a two-wheel hand pushcart. Include small Download and Upload
arrows. White background, very fine navy outlines, flat translucent color patches,
subtle rounded card borders, generous whitespace. Settlements should feel like
little architectural sketches anchored to the lane, not 3D game worlds.
Small footer: Illustrative data. Total data builds the city; live traffic delivers.
